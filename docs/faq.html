When should I apply for my residence permit?

You should apply for your residence permit as soon as you receive your notification of admission and pay your first tuition fee installment.

Can I work while studying on a student visa?

Yes, international students with a valid residence permit for studies in Sweden are allowed to work alongside their studies.

Do I need a visa if I am an EU citizen?

EU/EEA citizens have the right of residence in Sweden and do not need to apply for a residence permit.
